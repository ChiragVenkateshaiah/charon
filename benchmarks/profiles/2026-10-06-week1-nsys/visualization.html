Where a token's time goes

About half of Week 1's 29 ms per token is GPU work

The GPU does about the same work per token with or without the profiler: 14.5 ms, nearly all of it GEMV. The profiler only adds CPU time, and that extra time showed up one-for-one as GPU idle. So the step length is set by the host. Without the profiler, the same GPU work inside Week 1's measured 29.0 ms leaves about 14.5 ms of idle per token. That split agrees with the 53% utilization nvidia-smi reported in Week 1.

Where the scenario lands on the roofline

Hypothetical

L4 datasheet roofs: 300 GB/s memory, 121 TFLOP/s BF16. Arithmetic intensity is about 1 FLOP/byte per request in the batch, because the weights dominate the bytes moved. The filled dot is Week 1 as measured.

Model and assumptions
  • Step time = GPU idle + GEMV + other kernels (2.0 ms, derived from the trace and held fixed).
  • Per-request tokens/s = 1000 ÷ step ms. Total tokens/s = per-request × batch size.
  • Model FLOP/s = 2 × 1.54 B parameters × total tokens/s. Arithmetic intensity ≈ batch × 1 FLOP/byte.
  • Batching is idealized: step time does not grow with batch. That is an upper bound, not a forecast.
  • The Week 1 preset reproduces the measured 34.5 tokens/s at 29.0 ms per token. No other preset has been run.
